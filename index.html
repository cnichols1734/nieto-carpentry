About us.

Nieto Carpentry is owned and run by Enrique Nieto.

Enrique learned the trade on real job sites, working hands-on in framing, roofing, and remodeling. Over the years, that experience turned into a reputation for reliable work and honest pricing.

Projects are done by a small crew that shows up, does the work, and keeps the site clean. No middlemen. No surprises.
